# Agreements

## Signing starts the project, not acceptance
RespondToApplication no longer moves a project to in_progress. Accepting a student calls DraftAgreement and leaves the posting open; SignAgreement activates the agreement and starts the project when the SECOND signature lands. This follows the capstone PDF: the Terms and Agreements Form appears before collaboration begins.

The one-build-per-student cap still binds at acceptance, not at signing — a student who has been accepted is spoken for, and letting a second client accept them mid-negotiation would hand them two contracts to choose between.

Change requests supersede, they never edit. SupersedeAgreement marks the standing row superseded, points superseded_by forward and clones to version + 1 with no signatures. If terms could move after somebody signed, the contract log would be worthless.

## Agreements read as the SDPC Memorandum of Agreement; signed ones keep their wording
agreements.template (AgreementTemplate) decides the wording. SdpcMemorandum ('sdpc_moa', adopted 2026-10-03) is the default for every new agreement and every superseded version: sections I to X live in config('agreements.sdpc_memorandum'), and PresentAgreement fills the legend into the `moa` prop — CA1 = the Client Representative, the client ACCOUNT's name (the project's creator, else the business's owner account; never business_name or the profile's owner_name, the owner asked twice), CA2 = the Student Representative, the student account's name; the same two names fill Section III and the signature lines; the Description of Services = the project title. Every part of every section is numbered like the template's lists (config blocks are `numbered` items, an item may carry its own `lettered` list, as VIII.2 does); only the IV-VI lead-in lines stay unnumbered. The earlier school memorandum (Memorandum, config agreements.memorandum, `memorandum` prop) and Clauses are kept only for agreements somebody signed under them; migration 2026_10_03_033635 moved every unsigned draft to the SDPC memorandum. Memorandum and SdpcMemorandum share the `acknowledgements` keys; Clauses has clause_acknowledgements. Never switch a signed agreement's template, because a signature must stay with the words it was given for. The template's duplicated IX "Revisions and Modifications" paragraph is kept once.

## Both parties add to the memorandum; Section VII is required and NEVER adds a phase
Every addition is an agreement_requirements row (section = MemorandumSection, user_id = author). Section VII's services are rows in section 'services' with title = Objective and body = Scope (Agreement::services(), ServiceDescriptionController); they print as "**Objective**: scope", unlabelled. Since 2026-10-07 (owner) the phases of an SDPC agreement are fixed as two: Objective & Scope, then Turnover (config default_milestones). Section VII does NOT add phases: when the second signature activates the agreement, SeedServiceTasks makes each service ONE task in Objective & Scope, the objective its title and the scope its description. Objective & Scope has no dates and its tasks no deadlines: the client's Timeline is Turnover's dates only (AgreementController::scheduleMilestones writes null for every other phase of an SDPC agreement; agreements/show.tsx shows only the Turnover row and no Section VII services card), and the PM Gantt chart draws Turnover only. Migration 2026_10_07_001122 merged open and active agreements' Objective and Scope phases (each service's two tasks became one; a Scope task's submission moved onto its Objective task when that one had none, and was kept as its own item when both were handed over); completed agreements keep their three phases. agreement_tasks.title is text and SaveTaskRequest allows 2,000 characters. AgreementPolicy::addRequirements (either party, SDPC template, editable status, no signatures yet) and changeRequirement (the author only; an authorless row belongs to the client). SignAgreement::reasonItCannotBeSigned refuses both parties while Section VII is empty or a service lacks its objective or scope, then (SDPC) requires only Turnover's start and end dates and a Turnover of at least a month; older wordings still need every milestone's end date. PresentAgreement passes it as viewer.signingBlockedBy. SupersedeAgreement carries every requirement, title included.

SignAgreement no longer requires a milestone amount, only an end date. The pricing UI was removed on 2026-08-31 and milestones are seeded at 0, so requiring an amount meant no agreement made on the site could be signed. The MOA also leaves payment to the parties. The clause columns stay (nullable in SaveAgreementRequest, like the amount column).

## Turnover is the last phase, and its end is the final deadline
AgreementMilestone::isTurnover() is decided by POSITION (the highest on the agreement), never by name — clients rename phases. Agreement::turnoverPhase() / finalDeadline() (Turnover's scheduledEndsOn, the working schedule, not the signed ends_on) are the only way to read it.

Rules that hang off it: only a Turnover task may carry a due_on, on or before the final deadline, and need not; SaveTaskRequest drops (exclude) a due_on sent for any other phase, on every template (owner, 2026-10-07). UpdatePhaseScheduleRequest::after() lets Objective and Scope overlap, but Turnover may not overlap any other phase, runs at least one calendar month (TimelineWindow::isLongEnoughForTurnover, addMonthNoOverflow — checked on the agreement form, the schedule, a final-deadline ask and its approval, and signing), and its end cannot be dragged on the Gantt (phase-timeline.tsx disables that handle too). The final deadline moves only through an approved deadline request. Turnover's tasks are left out of every progress figure (Agreement::progressPhases). SummariseProgress exposes finalDeadline, daysToFinalDeadline and overdueTaskCount (AgreementTask::isOverdue), and flags each phase isTurnover so the dashboards draw no Turnover bar.

## A deadline, once set, moves only with the client's approval
AgreementTaskController::update refuses to change a due_on that is already set ("Ask for a change instead"). The student side (manageTasks: signer + teammates) asks through RequestDeadlineChange::forTask / forFinalDeadline, which stores a Pending DeadlineChangeRequest (task_id OR milestone_id, previous_on, proposed_on, reason). At most one pending ask per deadline. The client (verifyTasks) answers with DecideDeadlineChange::approve / decline; the student side may withdraw (destroy) while pending.

Approval re-checks the rules under a lock, because other dates may have moved since the ask: a task date must still be on or before the final deadline, and a new final deadline must still be after every task deadline and Turnover's start. Approving writes due_on or Turnover's planned end only — the signed starts_on/ends_on never change. Notifications: DeadlineChangeRequested (deadline.requested, to the business) and DeadlineChangeDecided (deadline.decided, to the student side); both have lines in PresentNotification. Both dashboard calendars show task deadlines and the dates asked for.

## Completing a project is the client's move and releases everyone
CompleteProject (POST agreements/{agreement}/completion, CompleteProjectRequest: rating required 1-5, feedback optional ≤2,000) locks the project row, refuses anything not InProgress, and sets the project AND every active agreement on it to Completed with completed_at. It is Completed, not Archived — Archived means a posting the client withdrew, and the landing page counts Completed as delivered work.

Nothing else needs undoing: isLockedToProject, Team::isBuilding and ProjectPolicy::create all read ProjectStatus::isUnfinished(), so the signer, the teammates and the business are free from that commit. Notifications go out after the commit: ProjectStatusChanged to the business, ProjectCompleted (project.completed) to Agreement::studentSide() — signer plus teammates.

Teammates are NOT added to the project chat automatically: group chats stay invite-only (.ai/rules/messaging.md) unless the testers decide otherwise.

## One open appeal at a time; meetings within a year; every timeline date is today to a year from today
Appeals (owner, 2026-10-09; replaces one-appeal-ever): an account may have one appeal waiting at a time; once it is granted or denied it may appeal again. users.restricted_at is stamped by User::booted() whenever the status becomes deactivated (or monitored), from any screen; User::currentAppeal() is the latest appeal unless it predates that stamp, so a resolved appeal from an earlier deactivation never resurfaces (settings card, admin monitoring list). FileAppeal checks for a pending appeal since restricted_at with a fresh query, never a cached relation. Both the settings form and the guest /appeal page go through it; account-appeal-card.tsx shows the waiting message with a disabled button while pending, and "Send a new appeal" under a decided one.

Meetings: OpenMeetingRequest takes scheduled_at after:now and before_or_equal now()+1 year (was 90 days); messaging/index.tsx meetingWindow() sets the same min/max on the datetime box.

Timeline dates (since 2026-10-03): every date set on the agreement or Project Management falls between today and today + 1 year, Singapore Time — App\Support\TimelineWindow and the App\Rules\WithinTimelineWindow rule, used by SaveAgreementRequest (every phase date), UpdatePhaseScheduleRequest (only dates that move, so a phase that started last week can still have its end moved), SaveTaskRequest (unless the task keeps the deadline it has) and RequestDeadlineChangeRequest; DecideDeadlineChange refuses to approve a date that has since left the window. The client's timeline form only writes dates (AgreementController::scheduleMilestones): it never renames, reorders, adds or drops a phase. resources/js/lib/calendar-days.ts (todayIso, timelineWindow, earliestTurnoverEnd) gives the date boxes the same bounds.

## Agreement references go one past the highest used, never the count
DraftAgreement::nextReference takes the highest SDPC-YYYY-NNN in use this year (withTrashed) + 1. It used to count distinct references + 1, and deleting an account hard-deletes its agreements: with only SDPC-2026-002 left, the count gave 002 again, the unique (reference, version) index threw, and every acceptance (client accepting an applicant, student accepting an invitation) was a 500 on sdpc.tech (2026-10-03). Never number anything here by counting rows. AgreementLifecycleTest pins both doors.

## In progress belongs to the agreement: nothing else may move it
From the second signature a project's status is the agreement's, and CompleteProject refuses anything not InProgress. Three buttons used to knock a running build out of it, leaving the client with "Only a project in progress can be completed" on finished work (sdpc.tech, 2026-10-04): the client's Archive project, the admin queue's Reopen/Close, and Close posting on a report. Each now asks ProjectStatus: ProjectPolicy::archive needs isArchivable() (draft, pending review, open, closed), and AdminPostingController::update plus ResolveIssueRequest need isModeratable() (pending review, open, closed). The UI hides the buttons (project.isArchivable, posting isModeratable, IssueResolution::optionsFor(Issue::postingCanBeClosed())). Migration 2026_10_04_015204 put every project with an active agreement back to in_progress. Any new status-writing path must ask the same question.

## Completing is rating; ratings are permanent and count the build for signer and teammates
Since 2026-10-09 (owner) CompleteProject takes the client's 1-5 rating and optional feedback (CompleteProjectDialog: stars + feedback, then a second "permanent once sent" confirm). For every agreement completed it writes a project_ratings row per student on Agreement::studentSide() (signer AND teammates), bumps each student_profiles.completed_projects_count, and recalculates rating_average and ratings_count. Rows keep project_title and client_name so they survive the project or business being deleted; ProjectRating refuses updates and there is no edit/delete route. Before this nothing wrote rating_average or completed_projects_count; the 2026_10_09_024956 migration reset them to real values (completed agreements, no ratings). StudentProfileFactory now starts both at 0 — use experienced() when a test needs a student with a finished project.

## The Project Extension Addendum is a second document, never a new MOA version
Since 2026-10-10 (owner) the client's "Project extension" button (beside Complete project on Turnover) opens an Addendum (table `addenda`) via RequestProjectExtension: only at Agreement::progress() >= Addendum::EXTENSION_THRESHOLD (80) and only one open (draft/awaiting/active) per agreement; references are <MOA ref>-A<max sequence + 1>. The memorandum is never superseded or edited. Its legend is the PDF's, the REVERSE of the MOA's: CA1 = Contracting Agency = student team lead, CA2 = Customer Agency = client representative (project creator, else business owner). Both parties edit only Section II (addendum_services: objective + scope, author-only changes) and Section IV's total_amount (whole pesos, 100..20000; Milestone 1 = 30%, Milestone 2 = rest, in centavos). Nobody signs (SignAddendum::reasonItCannotBeSigned) without a service, an amount, and BOTH representatives' GCash in Settings; each signature snapshots that side's GCash (encrypted). The second signature writes the two addendum_payments. SettleAddendumPayment (idempotent, row-locked) is the only way a milestone becomes paid: M1 seeds each service as an Objective & Scope task with addendum_id; M2 needs every such task submitted/verified and completes the addendum. CompleteProject refuses while an addendum is open.
